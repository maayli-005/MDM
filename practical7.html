College Help Portal - FAQs

USN: CM25090
1. How can I find my classroom?
You can check your classroom and timetable details on the student portal or department notice board.
2. What are the college library timings?
The library is generally open during regular college working hours. Check the library notice board for the exact timings.
3. How do I join a student club?
Students can join clubs by registering during club orientation or contacting the faculty coordinator of the club.
4. Where can I see the academic calendar?
The academic calendar can be viewed on the college website or obtained from the department office.
5. How can I request a change in my timetable?
Contact your department coordinator and submit a request explaining the reason for the timetable change.
6. How can I get information about internships?
Internship opportunities are usually shared by the placement cell, department faculty, and student portal.
